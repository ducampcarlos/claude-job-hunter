## Story: fixed a failing mail server after a migration
- **Situation:** after moving servers, outgoing mail started bouncing.
- **Task:** restore delivery before the client's launch.
- **Action:** traced the failure to an SMTP certificate verification error, reissued the certificate, verified SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
- **Result:** delivery restored the same day; documented a checklist for future migrations.
- **Tags:** servers, email, troubleshooting, communication
